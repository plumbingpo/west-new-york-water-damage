Roof Leak Water Damage in West New York: What to Know

Where you see the stain and where the rain gets in are usually two different places. Water enters at a raised shingle, a split in the flashing or a worn skylight seal, follows the rafters and decking downhill, and finally lands on the insulation. By the time a brown ring shows on the ceiling, the attic above it has often been wet for a while. Locating all of the moisture and drying it is our job.

Our trade is water damage restoration, and roofing is a separate one. Inside the home, our team takes out saturated insulation, dries the wood and drywall, treats any mold and repairs the ceiling. When the roof is still letting water through, a temporary tarp is something we can install, and we will coordinate with your roofer so the repair comes before the ceiling is closed.

Do This First

What to Do Right Now

  1. Shut Off Wet CircuitsWater coming through a ceiling fan, a light or close to an outlet means that circuit should be turned off at the panel, and the fixture should not be touched.
  2. Contain the WaterFurniture, electronics and rugs should be moved aside, with buckets placed wherever water is falling. Plastic or towels spread on the floor will keep it from soaking.
  3. Relieve a Ceiling BulgeWhen water pools in a bubble overhead, pierce the low point with a screwdriver while standing clear, and let it run into a bucket.
  4. Stay Off the RoofClimbing on slick shingles or crawling around a dark attic is not worth the risk. From indoors, mark where the water enters, and let professionals handle everything else.
  5. Photograph the DamageBefore anything is moved or discarded, get video and photos of the attic, the ceiling and damaged items, and then phone us so drying can begin.

The Usual Culprits

What Causes It

  • Popped or exposed roofing nails, each one a tiny opening for rain.
  • Missing or broken shingles, often after high wind, with bare underlayment beneath.
  • Holes and fractures left in the roof after a branch or hailstones hit it.
  • Clogged gutters that back water up under the shingles at the roof edge.
  • Flashing that has rusted, lifted or pulled away around chimneys, vents and walls.
Why Speed Matters

What Happens if You Wait

Ceiling Fixtures and Wiring

Because fixtures are holes in the drywall, a leak often shows up at a light or fan first. Keep that circuit off until someone has inspected it, since damp wiring may corrode or short out.

Drywall Ceilings

A ceiling panel left damp will discolor, bow and weaken. The tape releases along cracked seams, and with enough weight a section can come down suddenly.

Roof Framing

Wood in the roof structure that is soaked over and over darkens, swells and softens. Rot in these members weakens the structure that holds the roof up.

Insulation in the Attic

Wet insulation mats down, stops insulating and holds water against the ceiling drywall for days. Fiberglass can sometimes be dried. Removal is the usual answer for wet cellulose.

Coverage

Insurance and Roof Leak Water Damage

When a leak happens suddenly and by accident, for example a branch going through the roof or wind stripping shingles, many homeowners policies pay for the water damage inside. Leaks blamed on age, wear or skipped maintenance are usually denied, and the roof repair itself may be handled differently from the interior. Our team gives your adjuster photos and moisture readings, yet what is covered comes down to your policy, so check it or talk to your agent.

Pricing

What Affects the Cost

  • How easy the attic is to reach, how high the ceilings are, and if the roof needs a tarp before work begins.
  • How long the roof leaked before it was found, and any mold growth.
  • Drywall removal: how much of the ceiling and walls is beyond saving.
  • How much attic insulation is wet and whether it has to be removed.
