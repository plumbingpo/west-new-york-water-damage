Of all the problems an owner can have, sewage coming up through the drains ranks among the most dangerous. Anyone who comes in contact with contaminated material is exposed to parasites, bacteria and viruses, among them giardia, E. coli, salmonella and hepatitis, all carried in raw sewage and all dangerous. West New York Restoration provides emergency sewage cleanup throughout West New York, responding 24/7 with properly trained technicians, appropriate personal protective equipment and EPA-registered disinfectants to decontaminate your property safely.

Sewage damage is classified as Category 3 (black water), the most contaminated and dangerous category of water damage. That rating calls for the strictest protocols: containment barriers go up, crews wear full protective equipment, every porous material the sewage touched is removed, and the surfaces that stay are disinfected thoroughly. We work to the IICRC S500 standard that governs Category 3 losses, with the goal of returning your property to a condition safe to occupy.

The cleanup process involves removing all standing sewage, extracting contaminated water from structural materials, removing and properly disposing of every porous material that contacted sewage (carpet, padding, drywall, insulation), thoroughly disinfecting hard surfaces, applying antimicrobial treatments and completing structural drying to prevent secondary mold growth. To finish, thermal foggers and hydroxyl generators of industrial grade deodorize the space so the sewage smell does not linger.

Signs You Need Sewage Cleanup in West New York

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Watch for these telltale signs that professional help is needed:

  • Toilet water with waste in it soaking into carpet or running across the floor
  • Sluggish drainage at all fixtures, a sign the main line is blocking up
  • Running a sink or washer and hearing a toilet or nearby drain gurgle
  • Sewage or dark water backing up through floor drains, toilets or tubs
  • Strong sewage or rotten egg odors coming from drains or lower levels
  • Foul-smelling moisture showing up on flooring or along the walls
  • Unexplained illness or gastrointestinal symptoms among household members

The Benefits of Professional Sewage Cleanup

Sewage cleanup is not something any homeowner should attempt. The biological hazards in raw sewage require specialized training, proper personal protective equipment (Tyvek suits, respirators, protective gloves and boots) and EPA-registered disinfection products that are not available to consumers. People exposed to the pathogens in sewage can become seriously ill with skin and respiratory infections, hepatitis or gastrointestinal disease.

Disposal matters as well, since everything the sewage contaminated has to be handled as biohazard waste. Putting carpet, insulation or drywall soaked with sewage out with household trash breaks the law and endangers sanitation workers and your community. Licensed biohazard disposal facilities take this waste from certified restoration firms, which keep the required protocols in place.

Sewage Cleanup Prevention Tips

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • Use drain strainers in showers and sinks to catch hair and debris
  • A drain that is slowing down should be dealt with early, while it is still partly open
  • Toilet paper is the only thing to flush, and that rules out wipes sold as flushable, which still clog
  • Route sump pump and gutter water away from the sewer line, which can back up when storm flow overloads it
  • Never pour grease, oil or food waste down your drains, since these cause blockages
  • Install a backwater valve on your sewer connection to prevent backups from the municipal system
