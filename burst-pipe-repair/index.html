Few water damage events are as common, or as potentially devastating to a property, as a pipe that bursts. One broken line can put out 4 to 8 gallons every minute, so 240 to 480 gallons an hour may be running through floors, ceilings and walls. The trigger may be a hard freeze, corroded or aging lines, high pressure or physical damage, but the need is the same: act immediately so water does less harm to the structure, the floors, your possessions and the building's systems.

West New York Restoration provides 24/7 emergency response for burst pipe water damage throughout West New York and surrounding areas. Structural drying comes next and covers the wet drywall, the framing, the insulation, the subfloor and the finished flooring above it. Gravity carries the water through walls, floors and ceilings, so damage from one burst commonly shows up in several rooms and on different floors. We trace where the water migrated with thermal cameras and moisture detection tools, find each affected area and dry it properly, heading off the mold and structural decay that follow hidden moisture.

How to Prevent Burst Pipe Repair Problems

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Here are our expert recommendations:

  • Disconnect garden hoses and shut off outdoor faucets before the first freeze
  • Insulate exposed pipes in unheated areas such as attics, garages and crawl spaces
  • When it freezes, let taps drip and open the cabinets under sinks to let warm air reach the pipes
  • Never shut the furnace off completely during a winter trip, or pipes inside may freeze
  • Pressure higher than 80 PSI wears on pipes and their fittings, so test yours from time to time
  • Find the main shutoff valve now and practice closing it so you can do it fast

Signs You Need Burst Pipe Repair in West New York

Recognizing the warning signs early can save you time, money, and prevent significant damage to your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Tap water that turns brown, yellow or cloudy
  • In a cold snap, exposed lines that look frosted or frozen over
  • Unexplained standing water along baseboards or in the middle of a room
  • A fixture that stops flowing when it is below freezing outside
  • A spray or steady drip coming off pipework you can see
  • Hearing water move through the house while nothing is turned on
  • A sudden drop in water pressure throughout your property

Why Hire a Professional for Burst Pipe Repair?

What you can see after a pipe bursts is usually only part of the damage. Materials get saturated in spots that can stay undiscovered for days or weeks, because the water runs along conduits, joists, floor assemblies and the insides of walls. At that point mold can already be growing out of sight, and the structure may have lost a significant amount of strength. Professional restoration technicians use thermal imaging cameras and moisture meters to trace water migration and identify all affected areas immediately.

Professional-grade extraction and drying equipment also works on a completely different scale than consumer tools. A truck-mounted unit clears water at hundreds of times the speed of a shop vac, while a commercial dehumidifier takes 10 to 20 times more moisture out of the air than a household model. That speed and power are essential when you are racing the 24 to 48 hour window before mold growth begins.

Your Burst Pipe Repair Questions Answered

Pipes burst because of a freeze, old plumbing, corrosion, too much water pressure or a physical impact. Anything higher than 80 PSI is hard on your plumbing. Old galvanized steel and polybutylene pipes are also more likely to fail without warning. A licensed plumber can inspect your system for wear and corrosion.
Water extraction takes 2 to 6 hours. After that, expect 3 to 5 days of structural drying in most cases. Depending on how extensive the damage is, full restoration with flooring, paint and drywall repairs can take 1 to 3 weeks. Each customer receives a detailed schedule for their own job.
Water rarely stays where the pipe broke. It runs downhill inside walls, under floors and across ceilings. It can also travel along electrical conduits and between ceiling joists. Our thermal cameras and moisture detection tools show where it went, so each wet area is found and dried.
Expect 240 to 480 gallons or more every hour from a pipe that has burst. It does not take long for the bill to reach thousands of dollars once floors, walls, ceilings and your possessions get wet. The longer the water flows, the more extensive and costly the damage. Immediate response is critical.
Sudden, accidental water damage from a burst pipe is covered under most homeowner's policies, usually along with extraction, drying and repairs, subject to your policy terms. The pipe repair itself may or may not be covered, depending on your policy. We provide complete documentation for your claim.
